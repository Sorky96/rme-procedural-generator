# G4-C — style target from maps and generator-parameter fitting, 2026-11-08

**PARTIAL, largely negative result.** The mechanism (target from training maps, deterministic SurfaceTuning fit, profiles) works and is repeatable. On the available data **the fit does not generalize to other map families**: it improves training-map fit, but is usually worse than manual parameters on a validation family. There is no test-set assessment yet (G4-F); no test values were calculated or viewed.

## Included

- style_profile: g4-profile/2 schema (target and tuning added), targetFromStats, buildTarget (each family equally weighted and maps within a family equally weighted; a map counts above the natural-tile threshold), serializeStats/parseStats (g4-kindstats/1).
- style_fit: SurfaceKind → StyleKind projection (Lake and River = Water), statsOfSurface, evaluateTuning (sum over seeds), distance (L1 in ppm: five-kind shares + 25 adjacency cells), fitTuning (coordinate descent over fixed grids for seven parameters: lake, rock, dirt, shoreWidth, riverCount, transitionWidth, featureSize; three others fixed), selectStep (step with lowest validation distance).
- G3 probe writes kind-stats-surface.txt and kind-stats-underground.txt (accepted G4-B mapping); runner has --keymap. style_fit builds a training target per fold, selects a validation step, does not read test data, and writes fold-N.profile, profile-all.profile and fit-report.json.
- Tests test_procedural_style_profile and test_procedural_style_fit PASS (including reachable target: fit approaches a target generated by known tuning at least 2× closer; training distance does not grow between runs; optimal start is unchanged; repeatability; cancellation). Two style_fit series (17 s each) produced byte-identical files. The G4-A split is unchanged after refactoring the generality threshold.

- Fitting lowers training-target distance 2–10×, but validation improves only 3–7% (M05 and M06 folds) and **worsens twice** in M01 and winter folds (baseline wins when selected by validation).
- Fitted parameters reach grid edges (lake 300‰, rock 320‰, 8 rivers, transitionWidth 16 000), showing share-distribution fitting without appearance control. No visual check.
- Two complete runs give the same result (deterministic), but there are no uncertainty intervals across seeds or maps. They cannot be meaningfully reported for single-map folds.

## Why this does not generalize (unverified hypotheses)

Families are different styles (M06 world coast, grassy M05, mountain winter M11, dirt-only M14), and a family-weighted average describes none of them. The training target is a mixture. There is no single shared distribution to improve in the sense of approximating a foreign family.

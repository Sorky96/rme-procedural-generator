# G6 — D23: occupied z+1 with a house and spawn, one Undo (2026-11-19)

Criteria: [G6_METRICS.md](G6_METRICS.md), “D23 — occupied lower floor regression and one Undo”. No production code was changed. The reproduction found no defect.

## Probe

`occupiedFixtureCases` in `tests/test_procedural_surface_resolver.cpp` (the `--surface` path, with marker `OCCUPIED_Z1_FIXTURE_PASS` added to `g5_run_suite.py`). Each case first prepares an empty map to identify the z+1 cells that the generator would excavate, either cave or dungeon floor. Those cells are occupied on a second, fresh OTBM 5 map with a 4 × 4 house containing ground with action id and text, house id, PZ flag, zone and house exit, plus one monster spawn cell with radius 2, a “Rat” creature and a waypoint. The probe then runs `prepareTerrain` and `validateTerrainTarget` (Preview), `commitTerrainPreview` (Place), one Undo and one Redo. After every step, the same tiles (`Tile::deepCopy` and `snapshotTilesEqual`), the house (name, rent, exit and tile count), spawn list, waypoint and zone index must match. Both floors (z and z+1) must equal the pre-Place image after Undo and the post-Place image after Redo. Place must create exactly one history entry.

## Consumer and spawn protection

RME round-trip preserves legacy XML including radius=3 and direction. The current consumer reads Rat, position 160:160:7, and 90 s, but conversion creates fixed zone 158:158:7…162:162:7 with padding 2 and no legacy direction. **Radius/direction preservation and full semantic compatibility with this consumer did not pass.** Production ParseZoneEntry verification includes rereading TOML without activating monsters.

This difference is not data loss in the required RME round-trip. G0 requires an I/O audit and a recorded consumer result. Its gate is build + integration points + test-map round-trip and does not require deployment to an OTS world. The G0 audit is therefore complete, while consumer limitations remain open for future OTS compatibility. Gate criteria were not changed. A future generator must preserve all existing spawn data by default rather than treating lossy conversion as protection. Automatic migration to Zones and spawn generation require a separate consumer contract and test.
